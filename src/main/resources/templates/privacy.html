Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026 Applies during the closed beta

Komm is built so that your community's conversations live on hardware your community controls. This page explains what the Komm hub - the service behind kommvoice.com - does and does not know about you.

01 Who is responsible

The official Komm hub and this website are operated by Operator [operator name not configured] (“we”), who decides why and how your personal data is processed by the hub. You can reach us at contact [contact email not configured].

Komm has three parts: the desktop client, the self-hosted server that each community runs, and the hub. This policy covers the official hub, this website, and the account services the client uses from the hub. It does not cover community servers - see section 03. Anyone can run their own hub; if you use one, its operator's policy applies, not this one.

02 What the hub stores

DataWhat it includesWhy
Account Username, email address, password (stored only as a salted bcrypt hash - we cannot read it), role, profile picture, status, status message and emoji, last-online time, voice mute/deafen indicators, your direct-message privacy setting, the invite key you registered with. To create and run your account.

Direct messages are stored on the hub and are not end-to-end encrypted. Keep that in mind before sending anything you would not want a hub operator to be technically able to read.

03 What stays on community servers

Channel messages, files, soundboard audio, voice, video and screen sharing of a community are handled by that community's own server, on a machine its owner controls. The hub never receives this content.

  • Voice, video and screen sharing are end-to-end encrypted between participants, so even the server's media engine only handles ciphertext.
  • Text messages and files in a community are not end-to-end encrypted. They are stored on the community server, and its owner and administrators can access them.
  • When you connect to a server, its owner learns your Komm username and your IP address, as with any internet service.

Community servers are run by independent people. They are responsible for what they store and how they handle it, and may set their own rules. Only join communities whose owners you trust.

04 How we use data, and why we may

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, the legal bases we rely on are:

  • Providing the service you asked for (contract): your account, sign-in, friends, direct messages and the server directory.
  • Security and abuse prevention (legitimate interests): rate limiting, logs, enforcing the Terms, and revoking certificates of servers that abuse the network.
  • Email you need (contract / legitimate interests): verification and password-reset messages. We don't send marketing email.
  • Legal obligations, where the law requires us to keep or disclose something.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or build advertising profiles.

05 Who else is involved

  • Email delivery. Verification, password-reset and beta-key emails go through an SMTP email provider, which therefore sees the recipient address and the message.
  • GIF search. When you search for GIFs, the hub sends your search term to Klipy. The request is made by our server, so Klipy doesn't receive your IP address from us.
  • Hosting. The hub runs on infrastructure rented from a hosting provider, which processes data on our behalf.
  • Website fonts. This website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads. We use no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Authorities. We disclose data only when legally required to.

06 Cookies

We use only the cookies the site needs to work: a session cookie and a CSRF token for signing in on the website, and an HttpOnly cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. The desktop client keeps your settings and sign-in tokens on your own computer.

07 How long we keep it

  • Account data and direct messages: while your account exists. You can delete a conversation yourself; the rest is removed when your account is deleted.
  • Unverified accounts that never confirm their email are deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Sign-in tokens expire after 30 days and when you sign out.
  • Logs are rotated and kept at most 30 days.

08 Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and receive a copy of it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email us at contact [contact email not configured] from the address on your account. There is no self-service account deletion yet, so deletion is done on request. For anything stored on a community server, ask that server's owner.

09 Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions use signed short-lived tokens, servers authenticate to the hub with certificates it issues, client connections to servers are encrypted with TLS, and voice, video and screen sharing are end-to-end encrypted. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will tell you as the law requires. See the security overview.

10 International transfers

Depending on where the hub and its providers are located and where you are, your data may be processed in another country. Where the law requires it, we rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

11 Children

Komm is not meant for anyone under 16. If we learn that an account belongs to a younger child, we will delete it.

12 Changes

Komm is in closed beta and its features, and so the data it handles, may change. When this policy changes we update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell registered users by email.
